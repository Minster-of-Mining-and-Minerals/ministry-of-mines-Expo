Western Zone

Located in western Ethiopia, the West Zone is home to low grade Volcanosedimentary rocks, high grade Gneisses and Intrusives
This zone is particulary known for its rich deposits of Coal and Iron Ore, and gemstones such as Opal, Amethyst and Ruby
