Located in western Ethiopia, the West Zone is home to low grade Volcano-Sedimentary rocks, high grade Gneisses and Intrusives.
This zone is particulary known for its rich deposits of Gold and Base Metals such as Copper, Coal, Marble, Granite and Iron.
